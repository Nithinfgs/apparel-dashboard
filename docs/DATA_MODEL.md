# DATA_MODEL.md — Texcroft OS

All tables use `id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid()`,
`created_at timestamptz default now()`, `updated_at timestamptz default now()`
unless noted. Foreign keys are `uuid references <table>(id)`. This document is
the source of truth for both the target Supabase schema and the current
in-memory seed shapes in `src/types/` / `src/lib/seed/`.

## Identity & access

### profiles
1:1 with `auth.users`.
- `id` (= auth.users.id)
- `full_name`, `email`, `avatar_url`
- `role`: `admin | management | merchandiser | sourcing_manager |
  production_manager | qc_inspector | finance | factory_partner | buyer`
- `buyer_id` (nullable, fk → buyers) — set for `buyer` role
- `factory_id` (nullable, fk → factories) — set for `factory_partner` role

### roles / permissions
Modeled in code (`src/lib/permissions/roles.ts`), not as DB tables — the role
set is fixed and small enough that a static map is more auditable than a
join. If custom per-tenant roles are ever required, promote this to
`roles`/`role_permissions` tables without changing the `can()` call sites.

## Sales

### buyers
`company_name`, `contact_name`, `email`, `phone`, `country`, `currency`
(`INR|USD|GBP|EUR`), `shipping_location`, `payment_terms`, `tax_details`,
`notes`, `logo_url`

### buyer_contacts
`buyer_id` fk, `name`, `role_title`, `email`, `phone`, `is_primary`

### enquiries
`enquiry_no`, `buyer_id` fk, `merchandiser_id` fk→profiles, `product_summary`,
`expected_quantity`, `target_price` (nullable — unset until costed, see
BUSINESS_RULES.md §20), `currency`, `delivery_deadline`, `status`: `new |
requirements_received | costing | quote_sent | negotiation | confirmed |
lost`, `notes`, `tech_pack_document_id` fk→documents (nullable),
`converted_order_id` fk→orders (nullable)

Public-website provenance (nullable — absent for internally-entered
enquiries): `source`: `internal | website_quote | design_lab`,
`contact_name`, `contact_email`, `contact_phone` (the submitter's own
details, kept alongside the resolved `buyer_id` since the buyer record may
be a newly created prospect), `size_breakdown` (JSON map of size → quantity),
`design_config` (JSON — see below). See BUSINESS_RULES.md §20.

**`design_config`** (present only when `source = design_lab`): `product_category`,
`garment_type`, `sleeve_style` (nullable), `base_colour_name`,
`base_colour_hex`, `front_artwork_name` / `back_artwork_name` (filenames only
— see note below), `has_custom_measurements`, `fabric_preference`,
`gsm_preference`, `print_method`, `embroidery_required`, `packaging_notes`.
Stored as a single JSON column so a merchandiser can reopen exactly what a
buyer configured (`ProductionIssue`-style "View Design" card), not a
lossy text summary.

Note: no file-storage backend is provisioned in this environment (§4), so
Design Lab artwork uploads are previewed client-side only and recorded here
by filename — the binary itself is not persisted. This is a documented
limitation, not simulated success; see docs/HANDOFF.md.

(nullable once converted), `style_id` fk, `version_number`,
`selling_price_per_piece`, `currency`, `status`: `draft | final`,
`created_by` fk→profiles

### costing_items
`costing_id` fk, `category`: `fabric | rib | thread | labels |
buttons_zippers | printing | embroidery | washing | cutting | stitching |

## Orders

### orders
`order_no` (e.g. `TC-2609-014`), `enquiry_id` fk (nullable),
`buyer_id` fk, `style_id` fk, `costing_id` fk, `po_number`,
`quantity`, `price_per_piece`, `currency`, `order_value` (generated),
`factory_id` fk, `stage`: `costing | sampling | sourcing | cutting |
stitching | finishing | quality | packing | dispatch | completed`,
`expected_dispatch_date`, `actual_dispatch_date`, `owner_id` fk→profiles
(merchandiser), `risk_level`: `low | medium | high | critical` (computed,
cached), `risk_reason` (computed, cached)

### order_items
`order_id` fk, `colour`, `size`, `quantity`

### order_milestones
`order_id` fk, `milestone_key`: `techpack_approved | fabric_ordered |
fabric_arrival | pp_sample | buyer_approval | cutting_start |
cutting_complete | stitching_complete | finishing_complete | final_qc |
packing | dispatch`, `planned_date`, `actual_date`,
`status`: `pending | in_progress | done | delayed`, `owner_id` fk→profiles

## Sampling

### samples
`order_id` fk (nullable, an enquiry may be sampled pre-order), `style_id` fk,
`sample_type`: `development | proto | fit | lab_dip | size_set | pp`,
`version_number`, `status`: `requested | in_development | sent |
buyer_review | changes_requested | approved | rejected`, `courier`,
`sent_date`, `buyer_response_date`, `comments`

### sample_approvals
`sample_id` fk, `approved_by_buyer_contact_id` fk→buyer_contacts,
`decision`: `approved | changes_requested | rejected`, `comments`, `decided_at`

**Business rule:** an order cannot move its `stage` to `stitching` (bulk
production) until it has a `pp` sample with `status = approved`. Enforced by
`canEnterBulkProduction()` in `src/lib/calculations/production.ts`.

## Sourcing

### materials
`sku`, `description`, `category`: `fabric | rib | thread | buttons | zippers
| labels | packaging | other`, `supplier_id` fk, `colour`, `gsm`,
`required_qty`, `ordered_qty`, `received_qty`, `allocated_qty`, `cost`,
`unit`, `expected_arrival`, `status`: `required | rfq | ordered | partial |
received | qc_hold`, `order_id` fk (which order this material is for)

`available_qty` is **computed**, never stored: `received_qty - allocated_qty`.

### suppliers
`name`, `location`, `contact_name`, `contact_email`, `contact_phone`,
`material_categories` (text[]), `lead_time_days`, `payment_terms`

### supplier_materials
`supplier_id` fk, `material_category`, `typical_rate`, `moq` — supports
"which suppliers can quote this" lookups without denormalizing onto materials.

### purchase_orders
`po_no`, `supplier_id` fk, `order_date`, `eta`, `status`: `draft | sent |
partial | received | closed`, `payment_status`: `pending | partial | paid`,
`total_value`, `currency`

### purchase_order_items
`purchase_order_id` fk, `material_id` fk, `quantity`, `rate`,
`received_quantity`

### material_receipts
`purchase_order_item_id` fk, `received_qty`, `received_date`, `received_by`
fk→profiles, `qc_hold` (bool), `notes`

## Manufacturing

### factories
`name`, `location`, `contact_name`, `contact_phone`,
`capabilities` (text[] of `knits|wovens|printing|embroidery|washing|finishing`),
`daily_capacity_pieces`

### factory_capabilities
Junction if multiple capability *levels* are needed later; for the current
scope `factories.capabilities` (array) is sufficient and this table is
reserved for future per-capability metadata (documented here so nobody
re-invents it as a denormalized field elsewhere).

### production_assignments
`order_id` fk, `factory_id` fk, `assigned_quantity`, `assigned_date`,
`status`: `active | completed | reassigned`

### production_entries
`production_assignment_id` fk, `order_id` fk (denormalized for fast
lookups), `factory_id` fk (denormalized), `stage`: `fabric_ready | cutting |
printing_embroidery | stitching | washing | finishing | qc | packing`,
`date`, `produced_qty`, `rejected_qty`, `reworked_qty`, `workers_count`
(nullable), `notes`, `photo_document_id` fk→documents (nullable),
`entered_by` fk→profiles

**These rows are the source of truth for production totals.** An order's
"Completed" quantity for a stage is `SUM(production_entries.produced_qty)
WHERE order_id = ? AND stage = ?`. Nothing else is allowed to mutate a
cumulative total directly — see `docs/BUSINESS_RULES.md`.

## Finance

### invoices
`invoice_no`, `order_id` fk, `buyer_id` fk (denormalized), `amount`,
`currency`, `issue_date`, `due_date`, `status`: `draft | sent | partially_paid
| paid | overdue`

### payments
`invoice_id` fk, `amount`, `currency`, `paid_date`, `method`, `reference`,
`recorded_by` fk→profiles

`invoices.outstanding_amount` is **computed**:
`amount - SUM(payments.amount WHERE invoice_id = ?)`.

## Cross-cutting

### documents
`entity_type`: `buyer | order | style | sample | purchase_order |
quality_inspection | dispatch | production_entry | quality_defect`,
`entity_id`, `name`, `file_url`, `mime_type`, `version` (nullable),
`uploaded_by` fk→profiles, `uploaded_at`

### notifications
`recipient_id` fk→profiles, `type`: `risk | material | sample | quality |
finance | dispatch | general`, `title`, `message`, `entity_type`,
`entity_id`, `read` (bool), `created_at`

### activity_logs
`actor_id` fk→profiles, `action` (human sentence, e.g. "updated dispatch
date"), `entity_type`, `entity_id`, `old_value` (jsonb, nullable),
`new_value` (jsonb, nullable), `created_at`

### inventory_items
`sku`, `category`: `raw_material | wip | finished_goods`, `material_id` fk
(nullable), `order_id` fk (nullable, for WIP/FG tied to an order), `on_hand_qty`
(computed from transactions, never written directly)

### inventory_transactions
`inventory_item_id` fk, `movement_type`: `purchase_receipt | allocation |
production_issue | return | adjustment | finished_production | dispatch`,
`quantity` (signed: negative for outflow), `reference_entity_type`,
`reference_entity_id`, `created_by` fk→profiles, `created_at`

`inventory_items.on_hand_qty = SUM(inventory_transactions.quantity)`. This is
the same "events are truth, totals are derived" rule used for production and
payments, applied consistently across the whole system.

### production_issues

`order_id` fk, `factory_id` fk, `stage`: reuses the existing
`production_stage` enum, `issue_type`: `material_shortage | machine_breakdown
| quality_defect | labour_shortage | fabric_defect | power_outage |
logistics_delay | other`, `description`, `severity`: reuses the existing
`risk_level` type (`low | medium | high | critical` — no separate
issue-severity enum), `owner_id` fk→profiles, `status`: `open | in_progress |
resolved | closed`, `resolution` (nullable), `attachment_document_id`
(nullable fk→documents, reuses the existing document entity rather than a
dedicated attachment table), `reported_by` fk→profiles, `created_at`,
`updated_at`.

Open/in-progress issues are read by `calculateOrderRisk` (BUSINESS_RULES.md
§16) and surfaced on Order 360 and the Action/Exception Center
(BUSINESS_RULES.md §18); they are not a parallel risk system.

### order_change_requests

`order_id` fk, `field`: `quantity | colour | measurements | artwork | trims |
packaging | delivery_date`, `old_value`, `new_value` (both stored as text —
the field being changed determines how the UI formats them), `requested_by`
fk→profiles, `reason`, `status`: `pending | approved | rejected`,
`approved_by` fk→profiles (nullable), `approved_at` (nullable),
`implementation_status`: `pending | implemented | not_applicable`,
`created_at`.

A row is never overwritten to reflect a later state — approval and
implementation are recorded as of a fixed request/decision (BUSINESS_RULES.md
§19), preserving revision history instead of mutating the order's own fields
in place.

```
orders *—1 factories
orders 1—* order_milestones

orders 1—* costings 1—* costing_items

orders 1—* production_assignments 1—* production_entries
orders 1—* quality_inspections 1—* quality_defects
orders 1—* packing_records
orders 1—* dispatches
orders 1—* invoices 1—* payments

  (entity_type, entity_id)
```
